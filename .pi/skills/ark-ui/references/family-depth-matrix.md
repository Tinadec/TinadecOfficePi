## Use this matrix

Choose a family from product semantics, then choose a depth from implementation coverage. Do not make every family use the Endfield rail, grid, and readout. At `complex`, every family needs a complete shared component system, but its shell, stage, content owner, instrumentation, and motion remain family-specific.

| Family | Primary content owner | Shell signature | Complex-level instrument |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ark | Current operation or selected dossier | Black edge shell + cyan active rule | Indexed deployment stage |
| Endfield | Current field task or logistics plan | Pale rail + charcoal dock + yellow wedge | Calibration / route matrix |
| Ex Astris | Journey, subject, or archival record | Midnight stage + slim archive rail | Orbital chronology |
| POPUCOM | Party readiness or selected challenge | Dotted dark cap + bright rounded field | Cooperative state track |
| Corporate | Project, role, or studio statement | Translucent header + monochrome media | Editorial project sequence |

## Ark

- `minimal`: retain the existing shell; add condensed hierarchy, a black/white neutral system, one cyan selection rule, and explicit focus.
- `moderate`: introduce one black edge dock, indexed section labels, and either blueprint lines or desaturated media—not both.
- `complex`: coordinate operation stage, dossier, news/archive list, navigation, dialogs, code/data, and responsive edge docking. Use two to four layers such as masked media, blueprint rule, large identifier, and status baseline.
- `maximal`: art-direct operation, operator, world, and media modes separately while preserving the black edge shell and cyan state grammar.
- Content test: remove any code, coordinate, or meter that does not change a decision or identify a real record.

## Endfield

- `minimal`: retain the existing shell; add technical type, off-white/charcoal tokens, one yellow action marker, and square focus.
- `moderate`: add a pale rail or black utility dock plus one segmented field layer.
- `complex`: coordinate logistics/field stage, route matrix, action strip, task status, dialogs, code/data, and responsive recomposition with two to four engineering layers.
- `maximal`: art-direct construction, exploration, operator, and report modes separately, driven by real task state.
- Content test: yellow marks action or selection; it never becomes a generic long-text background.

## Ex Astris

- `minimal`: retain the shell; introduce serif narrative contrast, midnight neutrals, and one aqua focus point.
- `moderate`: add a slim archive rail plus one quiet ring or masked ticker.
- `complex`: coordinate journey stage, subject dossier, chronology, notices, tabs, and responsive orbital recomposition. Use two to four layers such as star points, ring, masked horizon, and archival band.
- `maximal`: give journey, character, world, and notice modes unique constellations and section transitions driven by the selected record.
- Content test: invented glyphs are decoration only; every journey, timestamp, name, and action remains readable.

## POPUCOM

- `minimal`: retain the shell; introduce friendly heavy type, one rounded primary action, and a functional teammate/category color.
- `moderate`: add the dark dotted cap, offset shadow, and one floating shape group.
- `complex`: coordinate party readiness, challenge preview, player roles, lobby actions, rewards/status, and responsive stacking. Use two to four playful layers with short bounce/roll feedback.
- `maximal`: give lobby, challenge, wardrobe, and reward modes separate playful compositions whose colors respond to real selections.
- Content test: every bright color must represent player, puzzle, status, or action; keep long copy on quiet surfaces.

## Corporate

- `minimal`: retain the shell; introduce monochrome hierarchy and one acid-lime active rule.
- `moderate`: add a translucent dark header, image-led split, and quiet project metadata.
- `complex`: coordinate project sequence, studio statement, role categories, media, calls to action, and responsive editorial reflow. Use at most two persistent decorative layers.
- `maximal`: build bespoke project narratives and transitions. Do not add HUD instruments merely to reach the layer count.
- Content test: the work, role, or statement is always larger than the interface chrome.

## Cross-family checks

1. Switch the family while holding depth constant. The composition—not only color—must change.
2. Switch depth while holding family constant. Content and accessible names must remain stable while coverage, layering, motion, and responsive re-art-direction change.
3. At `complex`, include full navigation, one primary action, one meaningful state region, shared controls, focus, reduced motion, and portrait recomposition.
4. At `maximal`, require state-driven composition and performance fallbacks. Reject decoration-only escalation.
